Braised Rooster with hand made pasta

Description

The dish is popularized in greece and contains a slow cooked rooster in wine and tomato sauce and served with hand made twisted pasta

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil and fry the pieces of rooster until browned on all sides.
  2. Add the wine, tomatoes, the tomato paste dissolved in a little water, the cinnamon, cloves, salt and pepper.
  3. Simmer the rooster over a low heat until tender, about 1 hour.
  4. Add a little extra hot water while it cooks, if necessary.
  5. Boil the pasta in salted water according to instructions.
  6. Strain the pasta.
  7. Remove the rooster pieces from the saucepan, and dispose of the cinnamon stick and the cloves.
  8. Add the spaghetti to the saucepan and bring to the boil once before removing from heat.
  9. Stir to ensure sauce is evenly distributed.
  10. Serve the rooster with the spaghetti on a large serving dish, and sprinkle with grated cheese.
