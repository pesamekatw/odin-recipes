Chicken schnitzel

Description:

The chicken schnitzel is a austrian dish that contains a thin chicken breast cooked in high heat oil

Instructions

  1. Pounce the chicken until flat
  2. Add the chicken to the egg mixture
  3. Add the chicken in the bread crumps
  4. Fry the chicken at hight heat for couple minutes until brown
  5. Served it with a slice of lemon
