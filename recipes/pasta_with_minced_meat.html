Pasta with minced meat

Home

Description

The dish is a "remedy" in Greek households because of it's rich flavours and warmth.

  1. Saute the mince
  2. Remove the meat and add the carrot, onion, celery and tomato paste
  3. Add red wine to deglaze the pan
  4. Add the mince back with the vegetables and add the tomato sauce
  5. In slow heat cook until smooth
